Over the past two days, I would say I was genuinely displaying autistic behavior, and I think this has a direct correlation of my small mental breakdown over my assessments (I was quite literally raging and depressed for a short while). 

Ever since I started to record what's happening in my life again, or having the conscience that this blog is present to be precise, I begin to pay more close attention to the minor details of life, and was able to slowly appreciate these little things, even if a lot of other things are not happening according to what I've anticipated. For example, the interactions I had with my classmates and tutor in my statistics class, playing card games with my friend or even talking to tutors in my other classes were actually quite enjoyable, and these human interactions temporarily made me forget the many burdens caused by my assessments.

Let's get back to the autistic part. For the previous 2 days, I've been posting sticky notes on my desk, with two purposes in general:
1. Aim to finish this tasks by today (mostly academics)
2. If these events happen, I'd do a specific action (that might be a reward, or something I'd afraid to do so)

However, I've realised 90% of the time I was unable to complete these tasks. An interesting thought popped up this morning when I woke up: why am I setting these constraints to myself? I guess it's a kind of self-reinforcement, but at the same time it's holding me back to escape my comfort zone. 

I would say though, notwithstanding things at it's surface hasn't changed much, making the effort to make this website good has made me happier, and has made me shift to an attitude knowing 'yeah, it sucks' but at the same time 'it would get better'.
